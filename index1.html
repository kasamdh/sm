Meet Your Physical Therapist

Monika Pokhrel, Physical Therapist

At Source Motion Physical Therapy, we believe that true recovery happens through personalized, attentive, and evidence-informed treatment.

Monika Pokhrel combines compassionate care with deep clinical knowledge in human movement, musculoskeletal rehabilitation, and injury prevention. Whether you are recovering from surgery, managing chronic pain, or aiming to return to athletic performance, Monika works directly with you every step of the way.

  • Individualized 1-on-1 treatment plans
  • Functional movement evaluations & posture analysis
  • Target root causes, not just symptom relief
  • Patient education and empowering home exercise programs

Clinical Specializations & Services

Comprehensive physical therapy services customized to meet your recovery goals.

Orthopedic Rehabilitation

Therapy for joint, bone, and tendon issues including knee, hip, shoulder, back, and neck conditions.

Sports Injury Recovery

Targeted rehabilitation for athletes and active individuals looking to return to peak performance safely.

Manual Therapy

Hands-on joint mobilization and soft tissue techniques to decrease pain and restore range of motion.

Post-Surgical Care

Guided, structured recovery protocols following joint replacements, ACL reconstructions, or spinal surgeries.

Postural & Balance Training

Exercises targeting spinal alignment, core strength, gait training, and fall prevention.

Chronic Pain Management

Multimodal strategies to reduce long-term discomfort and improve daily functional independence.
