Meet Our Founder

Dr. Monika Pokhrel, PT, DPT

Founder & Owner, Source Motion Physical Therapy

Dr. Monika Pokhrel, PT, DPT is the Founder and Owner of Source Motion Physical Therapy. Originally from Nepal, she has proudly called North Carolina home for the past four years.

Her patient-centered approach begins with listening closely, understanding each person's goals, and creating a practical plan to support more comfortable, confident movement.

Dr. Monika Pokhrel, PT, DPT believes that successful physical therapy begins with understanding the whole person, not simply treating a symptom. She takes the time to learn about each patient's concerns, daily responsibilities, and the activities that matter most to them.

Care may include therapeutic exercise, movement training, hands-on manual therapy, dry needling when appropriate, strengthening, mobility training, patient education, and individualized home exercise programs. The goal is to help patients make meaningful, lasting improvements in how they move and live.

Dr. Monika Pokhrel, PT, DPT brings more than 13 years of clinical experience, supported by a Bachelor of Physiotherapy from Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences in India; a Master of Science in Rehabilitation Science from the University of Dhaka and the Centre for the Rehabilitation of the Paralysed (CRP) in Bangladesh, where she received a SAARC Scholarship and graduated at the top of her class; and a Doctor of Physical Therapy degree from The College of St. Scholastica in Minnesota.

Her career has taken her across India, Nepal, Bangladesh, New York, and North Carolina, where she has helped patients recover from injuries, surgeries, neurological conditions, pelvic health concerns, musculoskeletal conditions, and persistent pain. Her focus is to restore movement, build strength, improve function, and help each person return to the activities that matter most.

Dr. Monika Pokhrel, PT, DPT also provides individualized physical therapy support for people with cardiopulmonary concerns, including COPD. Her approach emphasizes functional mobility, strength, pacing, and practical strategies for daily activity while working in coordination with each client's medical care.

Dr. Monika Pokhrel, PT, DPT also supports people recovering from stroke with individualized gait, balance, strength, and functional mobility training. Care is paced to each person's goals, current abilities, and medical guidance.

Outside of the clinic, Dr. Monika Pokhrel, PT, DPT enjoys traveling, discovering new places, experiencing different cultures, and exploring new foods. Her international background and experiences have shaped her appreciation for people from all walks of life and continue to influence the compassionate, individualized approach she brings to patient care.

  • Home-based support and coordination with your medical team when appropriate

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions before your visit.

What is cash-based physical therapy?

Cash-based, or direct-pay, physical therapy means you pay the practice directly for your care. This keeps the focus on your goals and allows your plan of care to be shaped around your needs rather than insurance visit limits.

Do I need to handle insurance paperwork?

No. Source Motion Physical Therapy does not bill insurance or require you to manage insurance claims for your visits. Before scheduling, you can contact us for current rates and payment details.

Can I receive physical therapy at home?

Yes. Home-based care may be a helpful option when travel is difficult or when practicing movement in your own environment supports your goals. We can discuss whether home-based visits are an appropriate fit for you.

What concerns can physical therapy help address?

Physical therapy can support recovery and improve movement for concerns such as pain, injuries, post-surgical rehabilitation, balance, mobility, strength, pelvic health, and everyday activity goals. Your evaluation helps identify a care plan suited to you.

How is care made convenient for my schedule and needs?

Care is designed around your priorities, with one-on-one attention, practical home programs, and home-based service when appropriate. We will discuss visit options and a plan that fits your goals and daily routine.

What experience does Dr. Monika Pokhrel, PT, DPT bring to care?

Dr. Monika Pokhrel, PT, DPT brings more than 13 years of clinical experience and advanced training in rehabilitation. Her approach combines careful listening, movement-based treatment, education, and individualized planning to help patients work toward meaningful functional goals.
