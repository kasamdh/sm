Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions before your visit.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring any relevant medical information, your insurance details if applicable, and comfortable clothing that allows you to move easily.

Do I need a referral?

Referral requirements can depend on your insurance and treatment needs. Contact the clinic before booking so we can help clarify what is needed.

How long is a treatment session?

Session length can vary based on your evaluation and care plan. We will explain the expected visit format when you schedule.

How do insurance and payment work?

Coverage and payment options vary. Reach out before your appointment to discuss current rates, billing, and any questions about your situation.

Can virtual rehab coaching be right for me?

Virtual coaching may be appropriate for some patients and goals. Your therapist can help determine whether it fits your plan of care.
